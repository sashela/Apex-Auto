# Apex Automation Website

This is a simple static website for Apex Automation.

## Before publishing
1. Replace `hello@apexautomation.nz` with your real business email if different.
2. If you have an NZBN, legal business name, phone number or business address you want public, add them in the Contact / Business Information section.
3. Make sure the business name shown here matches the name used on your Stripe account.
4. Publish the site on your own domain or a public hosting URL.

## Easy hosting options
- Cloudflare Pages
- Netlify
- GitHub Pages
- Vercel

Upload both `index.html` and `styles.css`.

The site is intentionally simple, public, mobile-friendly and clear about what Apex Automation sells.
